# Bounty Board

Give your players a place to find jobs, check rewards, and keep track of their contracts.

## Features

- Create bounties, deliveries, exploration jobs, or your own mission types.
- Add portraits, rewards, tags, locations, and notes to each contract.
- Search and filter jobs, then change their status as the party makes progress.
- Open a contract as a handout or show it to everyone at the table.
- Keep unfinished contracts and GM notes private.
- Choose large or compact cards and pink, yellow, or green reward details in the module settings.

## Getting started

Install and enable **HoloSuite Core** and **Bounty Board**, then open the board from the HoloSuite launcher.

Choose **New Contract**, fill in the job details, and enable **Published to Players** when it's ready to share.
