## CyberCall 2.0.1

- CyberCall keeps its inbox when Foundry chat is cleared. Messages sent while no GM is connected stay in the outbox and are delivered when a GM returns, even if the sender has logged out.
- Answer, Decline, and End Call update the call immediately while history saves in the background.
- Player contacts show the assigned character's name and portrait. The `@` contact handle uses the Foundry username.

### Upgrading from 2.0.0

Have a GM connect and let CyberCall import the existing messages before clearing Foundry chat. Records that fail validation stay in chat for GM review. CyberCall keeps the newest 100 call events per world and retains text messages.

Conversation visibility in the CyberCall inbox follows the audience and GM setting. Foundry world journal data can be inspected by connected player clients, and GMs retain access to the world records.

Supports Foundry VTT 12–14. Requires HoloSuite Core.
