# HoloSuite Loot 1.2.0

- Adds native Savage Worlds Adventure Edition (SWADE) support. NPCs drop weapons, armor, shields, gear, and consumables in stack quantities. Abilities, Edges, Hindrances, skills, powers, ancestry, natural armor, and granted items stay off the loot list.
- Applies the SWADE filter to existing loot panels. Previously selected gear still transfers as a whole item, and existing pickup claims retain their recovery data.
- Recognizes the combat tracker's defeat state in encounters without an assigned scene. With **Automatic defeat detection** enabled, **Dead** NPCs and NPCs marked **Defeated** are lootable. This includes SWADE Extras that the combat tracker treats as defeated after Incapacitation.

Requires **HoloSuite Core** and targets Foundry V13–V14.
