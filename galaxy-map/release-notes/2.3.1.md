## Galaxy Map 2.3.1

- Fixed faction territory outlines for locations attached to orbits. Assigned locations now remain inside their faction's outline as they move.
- Territory outlines and labels follow orbital animation, map resizing, and dragging, including with reduced motion enabled.
- Hidden and undiscovered contacts remain excluded from player territory outlines.

Requires HoloSuite Core. Supports Foundry VTT v12–v14.
