## Galaxy Map 2.1.0

- Add one or more orbit rings around a system-map location. Attach another location to a ring, drag it into position, or animate it clockwise or counterclockwise at a chosen speed.
- Create nested orbits, such as a moon circling a planet that orbits a star. Routes follow moving markers, and the **Orbit Lines** toggle controls ring visibility.
- GM editing fields and dropdowns now use a readable system font.
