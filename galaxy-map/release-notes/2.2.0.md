## Galaxy Map 2.2.0

- Click **Factions** in the map toolbar to open the faction panel and show territory overlays. Select a faction's name on either tab to see its dossier, including its description, headquarters, and known controlled systems.
- Create major and minor factions. Linked minor factions appear under their parent with a connector line and do not draw territory regions. Major factions can show separate regions for distant colonies.
- Set each faction's prominence in the galaxy and in individual systems. The **Reputation** tab tracks each faction's standing toward the party.
- Rename, add, or remove prominence and reputation ratings under **Configure Settings → Galaxy Map**. Renaming preserves assignments. Factions assigned a removed rating display as **Unrated**.
- Turn off **Enable Factions** in the same settings to hide faction controls and territories for the world. Re-enable it to restore access to saved factions and ratings.
- Fixed a bug that could leave the party at its old location after travel or after the GM set the current system manually.

Requires HoloSuite Core. Supports Foundry VTT v12–v14.
