# Example planet textures

These three original AI-generated example textures are bundled to compare visual styles:

- `cartoon.png`: Acid Seas — purple oceans and bright green alien continents.
- `adventure.png`: Golden Frontier — painterly orange land and turquoise seas.
- `realistic.png`: Blue Marble — a naturalistic terrestrial world.

All are 2:1 equirectangular surface images. They are replaceable through the Planet Texture field; do not overwrite these module files for a custom campaign, since module updates may replace them. Store custom textures in world or shared asset folders instead.

These are illustrative presets, not scientific maps. They may show small generated seam or polar artifacts when rotated.
