# HoloSuite Core 1.4.0

- **Cassette Futurism** brings HoloSuite Terminal’s dark teal display, cream labels, and copper accents to Core. The HoloSuite theme has a rounded cyan bezel, and Space Police uses black controls with signal-yellow accents.
- **Compact, Datapad, and Computer** are available from launcher Settings. All themes fit all three sizes, and players can choose their own size when the GM sets a world-wide theme.
- **Use Themed App Colors** in Foundry’s HoloSuite Core module settings lets each user tint app icons and their hover and press glow. Apps use their own colors by default.
- The square **Close** button is beside the **What’s New** star. Foundry 12 now centers the star, left-aligns theme previews, and gives the size choices and app tiles consistent spacing.
- Theme bezels and hardware are editable SVGs with documented dimensions in `assets/device-styles/README.md`.
